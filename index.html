認識文物修復專業 Understanding the Conservation Profession

什麼是文物保護？ What is Artifact Conservation?

文物保護是一門結合科學、藝術與歷史的專業學科。修復師透過科學分析了解文物的材料特性與退化機制，運用專業技術進行穩定化處理與修復，同時尊重文物的歷史完整性與真實性。 Artifact conservation is a discipline combining science, art, and history. Conservators use scientific analysis to understand material properties and degradation mechanisms, applying specialized techniques for stabilization and restoration while respecting historical integrity and authenticity.

職業發展路徑 Career Pathways

在香港及國際上，文物修復師通常需具備相關碩士學位（如文物科學、保存修復等），並累積實習及專業經驗。就業方向包括博物館、文化機構、大學研究部門、私人修復工作室等。 In Hong Kong and internationally, conservators typically hold a relevant master's degree (e.g., in conservation science, heritage preservation) with practical internships. Career paths include museums, cultural institutions, university research departments, and private conservation studios.
